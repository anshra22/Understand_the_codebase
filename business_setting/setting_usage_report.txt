Business Settings: Complete Usage Report

This document provides a definitive map of every Business Setting ID, its purpose, and a complete list of every file in the application that reads that setting to change its behavior.

---
### **Category: 🧾 Invoice**
---

**1. Setting: Invoice Prefix**
   - **Purpose:** Sets a custom starting text for invoice numbers (e.g., "INV-2024-").
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sale_invoice_create_screen.dart`

**2. Setting: Terms & Conditions**
   - **Purpose:** Appends legal text to the bottom of all generated PDFs/Receipts.
   - **Used In:** `widgets/pdf_template_one.dart`, `widgets/pdf_template_two.dart`, `widgets/pdf_template_three.dart`

**3. Setting: Show Discount Column**
   - **Purpose:** Hides or unhides the discount input fields during the Sales flow.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sale_invoice_create_screen.dart`, `feature/sales/views/pos_screen.dart`

**4. Setting: Show Tax Column**
   - **Purpose:** Hides or unhides the GST/Tax calculation fields during the Sales flow.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sale_invoice_create_screen.dart`, `feature/sales/views/pos_screen.dart`

**5. Setting: Enable Invoice Note**
   - **Purpose:** Adds an extra "Notes" section to the generated PDF.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sales_invoice_screen.dart`

---
### **Category: 📦 Product**
---

**1. Setting: Custom Serial Label**
   - **Purpose:** Renames "Serial Number" globally (e.g., to "IMEI").
   - **Used In:** `feature/products/view/product_add.dart`, `feature/products/view/product_details.dart`, `feature/sales/views/sale_invoice_create_screen.dart`, `feature/sales/views/sales_product_screen.dart`, `feature/sales/views/pos_screen.dart`, `feature/purchase/view/purchase_product_screen.dart`, and all `*_return` screens.

**2. Setting: Enable Serial Tracking**
   - **Purpose:** Unlocks the ability to track and scan individual items.
   - **Used In:** `feature/products/view/product_add.dart`

**3. Setting: Want Bundles in Products**
   - **Purpose:** Unlocks the "Composite Item" feature to group products.
   - **Used In:** `feature/products/view/product_add.dart`

**4. Setting: Enable Product Variants**
   - **Purpose:** Unlocks the ability to add colors, sizes, etc., to a product.
   - **Used In:** `feature/products/view/product_add.dart`

**5. Setting: Enable Batched Products**
   - **Purpose:** Unlocks the ability to track items by batch number and expiry date.
   - **Used In:** `feature/products/view/product_add.dart`

**6. Setting: Quick Add to Cart**
   - **Purpose:** Bypasses the confirmation dialog in POS for faster sales.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sales_product_screen.dart`

---
### **Category: 🖨️ Printer**
---

**1. Setting: Invoice Template Selection**
   - **Purpose:** Chooses the visual design (Template 1, 2, 3, or 4) for the PDF.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sales_invoice_screen.dart`

**2. Setting: Thermal vs A4 Print Format**
   - **Purpose:** Switches the PDF engine to generate tiny 58mm receipts.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sales_invoice_screen.dart`

**3. Setting: Print on Save**
   - **Purpose:** Automatically triggers the Bluetooth printer dialog upon invoice creation.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/sale_invoice_create_screen.dart`

---
### **Category: ⚙️ Advance**
---

**1. Setting: POS Quick Actions**
   - **Purpose:** Enables fast-checkout buttons on the Point of Sale screen.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/pos_screen.dart`

**2. Setting: Auto Round-off**
   - **Purpose:** Automatically rounds final invoice totals to the nearest whole number.
   - **Used In:** `feature/sales/views/pos_screen.dart`

**3. Setting: Show Paid/Due Amount**
   - **Purpose:** Prints the remaining balance on the PDF invoice.
   - **Used In:** `widgets/pdf_template_two.dart`, `widgets/pdf_template_three.dart`

**4. Setting: Show MRP on PDF**
   - **Purpose:** Prints the "Max Retail Price" of an item on the invoice.
   - **Used In:** `widgets/pdf_template_one.dart`, `widgets/pdf_template_two.dart`, `widgets/pdf_template_three.dart`, and `sales_return` screens.

---
### **Category: 🏢 Business Details**
---

**1. Setting: Company Logo**
   - **Purpose:** Displays the company logo at the top of all PDFs and Estimates.
   - **Used In:** All `widgets/pdf_template_*.dart` and `estimate_template.dart` files.

**2. Setting: Authorized Signature**
   - **Purpose:** Displays a signature image at the bottom of official documents.
   - **Used In:** All `widgets/pdf_template_*.dart` and `estimate_template.dart` files.

**3. Setting: Company Name**
   - **Purpose:** Injects the business name into headers, PDFs, and payment links.
   - **Used In:** All `widgets/pdf_template_*.dart` and `estimate_template.dart` files.

**4. Setting: Address**
   - **Purpose:** Prints the physical location of the business on invoices.
   - **Used In:** All `widgets/pdf_template_*.dart` and `estimate_template.dart` files.

**5. Setting: GSTIN / Tax ID**
   - **Purpose:** Displays the legal tax registration number on invoices for compliance.
   - **Used In:** All `widgets/pdf_template_*.dart` and `estimate_template.dart` files.
